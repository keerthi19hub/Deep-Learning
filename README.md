## 1. Overview & Research Scope

As deep learning models increasingly influence clinical diagnostics, financial lending, and judicial sentencing, evaluating their societal and ethical consequences is paramount.

This repository hosts a formal academic research study:
- **Document:** `Assignment 4 _Ethical Considerations (DL).pdf`
- **Focus Areas:**
  - **Algorithmic Bias:** How historical disparities in training data propagate into production models.
  - **Explainability (XAI):** The opacity of deep neural networks and methods for post-hoc interpretation.
  - **Privacy:** Membership inference attacks and differential privacy defenses.
  - **Accountability:** Governance frameworks for responsible AI deployment.
